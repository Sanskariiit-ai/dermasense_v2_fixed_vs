General skin care

Gentle routines help many skin problems. Use lukewarm water, mild fragrance-free cleansers, and moisturise after bathing. Protect skin from sun with shade, clothing and a broad-spectrum sunscreen. Avoid scratching, picking and harsh scrubs. Introduce only one new product at a time, so you can tell which product causes a reaction. A patch test on a small area of skin helps identify sensitivity.

Hair loss has many causes, including stress, illness, nutrition, hormonal changes, scalp conditions and medicines. Patchy hair loss, scalp pain or scarring should be assessed by a doctor.

A rash can come from irritation, allergy, infection or medicine. Note when it started, what changed (new products, foods, medicines), whether it itches or hurts, and whether it is spreading.
