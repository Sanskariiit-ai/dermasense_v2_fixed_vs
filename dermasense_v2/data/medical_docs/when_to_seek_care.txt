When to seek medical care for a skin problem

Seek emergency help immediately for trouble breathing, swelling of the face, lips, tongue or throat, widespread blistering or peeling skin, blisters in the mouth or eyes, or a rash with high fever and feeling very unwell.

Seek urgent medical review for a skin area that is rapidly spreading, hot, swollen and painful, red streaks spreading from a wound, or pus with fever.

See a doctor soon for a mole or spot that changes in size, shape or colour, bleeds, or a sore that does not heal within a few weeks; a rash that lasts more than a couple of weeks; skin problems that interfere with sleep, work or mood; or any condition that does not improve with simple self-care.

This information is educational and cannot replace an examination by a qualified health professional.
