Rosacea

Rosacea is a long-term skin condition that mainly affects the central face. It causes persistent redness, episodes of flushing, and small visible blood vessels. Some people also get red bumps or pimple-like spots, and the skin may sting or burn. It is more often noticed in fair-skinned adults, though it can affect any skin tone.

Common triggers include sun exposure, heat, hot drinks, spicy food, alcohol, stress, and some skincare products.

General care: keep a diary to identify your triggers, use a broad-spectrum sunscreen daily, and choose gentle, fragrance-free skincare. Prescription creams, gels or tablets can help, so see a doctor or dermatologist for assessment. Eye symptoms such as redness, grittiness or pain can occur with rosacea and should be checked promptly. Swelling or thickening of the nose can develop over time without treatment.
